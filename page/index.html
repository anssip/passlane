passlane approvals

This is the passlane approval page: the Phase 0 approver before the iOS app exists (see the passlane repository and crates/passlane-approval/SPEC.md).

You normally land here through a link passlane printed:

  • /enroll#… — one-time enrollment of this device's ECDH key and passkey (passlane approvals enroll on the computer). The link material rides the URL fragment, which is never sent to this site.
  • /approve#… — an approval request sealed to the key enrolled here. Approve with the passkey, paste the reply back into the terminal.

No secrets reach this site's server: everything sensitive travels in the URL fragment and inside end-to-end encrypted envelopes whose keys are pinned between your terminal and this device at enrollment.
